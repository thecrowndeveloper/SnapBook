# OneCre8 Media — Draft Content Policy

**Status:** Working draft for legal review. Not legal advice; obtain Nigerian legal review before publication.

## Purpose and scope
Applies to clients, Creative Professionals, businesses, listings, portfolios, messages, reviews and material submitted to OneCre8 Media.

## Ownership, copyright and permissions
Users may upload only material they own or are authorized to use. Creative Professionals must obtain appropriate permissions and releases before publishing identifiable client images, videos, artwork, music or other protected material. Users must respond promptly to substantiated rights complaints.

## Privacy and consent
Do not publish private contact details, addresses, personal records or identifiable images/recordings without a lawful basis and appropriate consent. Do not expose confidential client information through portfolio uploads.

## Prohibited material and conduct
Prohibited content includes illegal or exploitative material, threats, harassment, discriminatory abuse, non-consensual intimate imagery, impersonation, scams, malware, deceptive listings, counterfeit goods, fraudulent payment requests and content that infringes others' rights.

## Marketplace accuracy
Listings must accurately describe services, products, equipment condition, rental terms, location, availability, price and additional fees. Do not misrepresent qualifications, identity or verification status.

## Reviews and ratings
Reviews must reflect a genuine completed transaction. Fake, purchased, retaliatory, self-authored or manipulated reviews are prohibited. Do not pressure clients to change honest reviews or offer undisclosed incentives for positive ratings.

## Off-platform circumvention
Users must not deceptively divert a transaction to evade applicable platform fees or safeguards. Legitimate pre-booking discussions and reasonable sharing of project requirements are allowed. Enforcement must be proportionate and transparent.

## Reporting and moderation
Users may report content, listings, reviews or accounts. OneCre8 may temporarily restrict material while assessing a report, preserve relevant transaction records, request clarification, remove violating content or restrict accounts in accordance with its Terms and applicable law.

## Appeals
Where reasonably possible, affected users will receive the reason for enforcement and a way to request review. Appeals should be assessed by an authorized person with an audit record.

## Safety and incidents
Users should report suspected fraud, threats, privacy incidents or unsafe conduct promptly. OneCre8 should maintain an incident-handling process and cooperate with lawful requests.

## Before launch
Add legal entity details, contact channel, notice-and-appeal timelines, data-retention rules, child-safety provisions and legally reviewed complaint procedures.
