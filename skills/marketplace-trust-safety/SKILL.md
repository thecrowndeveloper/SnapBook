# Marketplace Trust & Safety

## Research
- Identify abuse scenarios, impacted users, enforcement authority, existing reporting/moderation/appeal controls, and evidence retention constraints.
- Examine both malicious behavior and false-positive/retaliation risks.

## Plan
- Define policy, user-facing explanation, proportionate action, appeal path, access boundaries, auditability, and abuse-resistant tests.
- Do not create broad moderator access or collect unnecessary evidence.

## Implement
- Protect against spam, scams, impersonation, harassment, malicious uploads, review manipulation, booking abuse, and off-platform contact abuse where applicable.
- Enforce rules server-side; rate limit/report abuse; preserve privacy; log moderation actions with restricted access.
- Support fair review and appeals, avoid automatic irreversible punishment based on one unverified report.

## Gate
Verify positive and negative scenarios, moderation permissions, privacy, and appeal handling. Escalate policy/legal questions instead of inventing policy.
