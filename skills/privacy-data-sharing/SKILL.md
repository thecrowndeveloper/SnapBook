# Privacy, Data Protection & Sharing

Use Nigeria's NDPA 2023 and applicable NDPC guidance as key references for OneCre8; confirm current legal requirements for each specific processing activity. This skill is an engineering aid, not legal advice.

## Research
- Inventory personal data, data subjects, purposes, flows, recipients, processors, locations, retention, and access.
- Identify lawful basis and consent requirements where applicable; document uncertainties for legal review.
- Check public/private boundaries and whether each field is truly necessary.
- Consider breach impact, re-identification, children/vulnerable users, sensitive data, and cross-border transfers.

## Plan
- Apply privacy-by-design/default, purpose limitation, minimization, least privilege, transparency, retention limits, and secure disposal.
- Define user access/correction/deletion handling and exceptions (e.g., legal retention).
- Map sharing recipients, fields, purpose, authorization, safeguards, logging, and revocation.
- Consider DPIA/impact assessment where required or risk indicates.

## Implement
- Collect only required data; do not expose private contact details on public profiles.
- Restrict messages, booking records, attachments, support data, and verification records to authorized parties.
- Avoid placing personal data or secrets in logs, analytics, test fixtures, agent records, or prompts unnecessarily.
- Ensure notifications, exports, emails, admin screens, APIs, and third-party integrations use minimum necessary data.
- Document retention/deletion behavior and processor responsibilities.

## Gate
Review privacy impact, permissions, sharing, retention, and evidence before release. Do not infer that platform access equals permission to share data. Escalate legal interpretation to qualified counsel.
